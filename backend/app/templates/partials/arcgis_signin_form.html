Registering the Application
Register an OAuth application in your portal, add this site's callback URL to it as a redirect URI, then copy its App ID and App Secret here. Only members holding the required role can sign in; everyone else is refused after authenticating. Local accounts are unaffected and remain the way in if any of this is wrong.
Who Can Administer
Signing in and administering are separate. Every ArcGIS account arrives without staff status and is promoted by hand in the Django admin, because staff can edit the settings that hold this App Secret, the webhook secret and the SMTP password.
